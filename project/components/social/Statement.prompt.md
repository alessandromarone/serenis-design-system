Headline block inside a SocialSlide: Degular Display Semibold headline, optional Mona Sans supporting line, optional white "marker" label.

```jsx
<Statement sub="Le abbiamo chieste a voi 💜">Frasi della mia terapeuta che hanno alterato la chimica del mio cervello:</Statement>
<Statement marker="Ascoltare la rabbia">significa riconoscere che…</Statement>
```

- Sizes on the 1080 canvas: m 72px, l 88px, xl 110px; line-height 1.08.
- `marker` approximates the hand-lettered crayon label seen on posts (the real one is lettered by hand, violet on white). Swap in the real lettering when available.
- Headlines often end with ":" to lead into the carousel, or with a single 💜.
