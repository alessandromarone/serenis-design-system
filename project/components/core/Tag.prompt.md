Pill label for topics or short highlights on posts and cards.

```jsx
<Tag>Ansia</Tag>
<Tag tone="brand" size="l">Psicologia</Tag>
<Tag tone="white">1° colloquio gratuito</Tag>
```

- `soft` default (lavender); `brand` violet; `white` on violet grounds; `outline`; `dark`.
- Sentence case, 1–3 words.
