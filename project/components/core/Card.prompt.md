Rounded, mostly flat content block for tips, steps or service explainers.

```jsx
<Card tone="tint" eyebrow="Passo 1" title="Compili il questionario">Bastano pochi minuti.</Card>
<Card media={<Illustration name="omino-caffe" height={160} />} title="Una pausa vale">…</Card>
```

- Tones: `white` (hairline border), `tint` (lavender), `brand` (violet), `dark`.
- Flat by default; `elevated` adds the soft violet shadow.
