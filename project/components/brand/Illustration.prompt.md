Places an official Serenis illustration (Mino or a hand) by name; never draw new characters or hands.

```jsx
<Illustration name="omino-zoom" width={320} base="../../assets/illustrations/" />
<Illustration name="mano-17" height={240} />
```

- Omino set: zoom, annoiato, appoggiato, cade, caffe, cucu, centro, bici, 1/2 (with notepad).
- Hands: mano-16…23 (gestures, counting 1–5), batti-8 (high five), mano-gratis, mano-sconto, mano-alt (wave line).
- Line is always violet-500 on a violet-100 blob; place on white or violet-100 grounds.
- **Opaque PNGs** (white background baked in): omino-1, omino-2, omino-annoiato, omino-appoggiato, omino-appoggiato-2, omino-cade, omino-cucu, omino-zoom, omino-zoom-1. Use them only on white grounds or inside a white circle/rounded container. All hands, omino-caffe and the SVGs are transparent.
